Chapter 8: Love Is Gold

Written by Peter Piper Camelot And “The Rages Band”

Once upon a time there is a man came named Eliot The Gold Time. Elliot has a first home named “House Of Gold”. That’s why he named himself “Love Is Gold”. And for sure all of the money he spent is almost lost now before. So there are the times his house came on the rent to spent.

But the accident happen on why his money gets lost. Because all in all he buy things such as expensive like Ship, car etc. that is not necessary to buy. He don’t think first before he buy. So as taken the result he don’t pay the rent. Eliot get mad and said, why you there renters taken my house as a rent. Renters said, sorry it’s too late. Because now is the late time to pay your rent house. So regret and get out of the house because you don’t save money for the house.

Eliot The Gold Time betrays his own self because he has no shelter to do. So much last 50 years of finding. He regret that he must save money from the past to sustain his own house. But because of money he love. All of the dreams is fade away. As the story ends, Eliot died without future gold. Cause all also his jobs are gone because of buying expensive and excessive things that is not necessary to buy them all. And the cause of death of hunger.

Moral Lesson:

"Be practical, save money for the future use. Be assertive to buy only the important things which is relevant to finished the rents. And also don’t buy that is not important in future you love. Because death takes time. You don’t know when without gold. Think before you buy."
